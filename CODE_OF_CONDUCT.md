# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Spaces community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior include demonstrating empathy and kindness,
respecting differing viewpoints, accepting constructive feedback, taking
responsibility for mistakes, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or attention, trolling,
insults or personal attacks, public or private harassment, publishing another
person’s private information without permission, and other conduct reasonably
considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Community leaders may remove, edit, or reject contributions and interactions
that violate these standards. Report conduct concerns privately to the
repository maintainer through the contact method on the maintainer’s GitHub
profile, or use GitHub’s reporting tools when appropriate. Reports will be
reviewed promptly and with respect for the reporter’s privacy.

Corrective action may range from a private correction or warning to a temporary
or permanent ban, based on impact and repeated behavior.

## Scope and attribution

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when someone officially
represents the project in public.
